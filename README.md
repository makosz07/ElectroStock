# ElectroStock

**ElectroStock** is a free Android app for organizing and tracking electronic components, such as resistors, capacitors and other parts in your electronics inventory.

Keep track of the components you already own before buying the same parts again.

## Electronic component inventory

ElectroStock is designed for electronics hobbyists, makers and people who want a simple way to manage their electronic parts collection.

- Platform: Android
- Price: Free
- Developer: Macosh Apps
- Languages: Polish, English and Spanish
